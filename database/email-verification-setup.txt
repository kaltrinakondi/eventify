EMAIL VERIFICATION (required)

In Supabase Dashboard you MUST enable this or signup will be blocked by the app:

1) Authentication → Providers → Email
   → Enable “Confirm email” (ON)

2) Authentication → URL Configuration
   Site URL:
     https://eventify-olive.vercel.app
   Redirect URLs (add all):
     https://eventify-olive.vercel.app/verify-email.html
     https://eventify-olive.vercel.app/**
     http://localhost:5500/verify-email.html

After that:
- New users get a verification email
- They cannot sign in until they click the link
- The app also blocks unverified sessions client-side
