Our Story

Why we built Eventify

Eventify started with a simple idea: finding and organizing great events should be effortless. Whether you're planning a wedding, hosting a conference, or looking for the next concert to attend, Eventify brings organizers and attendees together on one modern, easy-to-use platform.

Today, thousands of organizers use Eventify to create, promote, and manage events of every size, while attendees discover new experiences, RSVP in seconds, and keep track of everything they love in one place.
